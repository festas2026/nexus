PRENOTA il tuo posto
With/in Floods & Lullaby for Floods
Mimmo Napolitano, Letizia Artioli, Lorenzo Setti

“Sale” _quadraphonic experience
(Lorenzo Setti aka ATŌMI)

Listening session inspired by the winter sounds of the Amalfi Coast, a critical reflection on the “tourist flood” that affects the area during the spring and summer months. This phenomenon alters not only the landscape, but also the society that inhabits it, leaving cultural and social desolation in its wake.

This is why the artist compares it to the ebb and flow of the tide, which inspired him to choose the title 'Sale' (Italian word that has a double meaning: salt – to rise), later included in the Various Artists compilation 'Within Flood', released by ATME Records to raise awareness of flooding and increasingly frequent, devastating climate-related disasters in a broad and metaphorical sense.
